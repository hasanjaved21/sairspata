Our Facilities

In/Out Door Games

Table tennis, carrom, cricket, badminton, and various indoor/outdoor activities for all ages.

Comfortable Rooms

Air-conditioned rooms with attached baths, comfortable bedding, and modern amenities for overnight stays.

Big Swimming Pool

Spacious crystal-clear swimming pool with separate kids' section, perfect for summer enjoyment.

Dedicated barbecue setup with grills, seating, and open-air dining space for delightful cookouts.

Fully equipped modern kitchen with appliances, utensils, and space for catering services.
